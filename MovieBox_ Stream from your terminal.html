Any movie, one word in your terminal. No waiting for a download.

Type moviebox-tui. Pick a movie. Press play.

Works in PowerShell on Windows and Terminal on Mac.

Stream straight into VLC

MovieBox hands the stream to VLC while it plays. Nothing gets saved to your disk, so there is nothing to clean up afterwards.

Subtitles come as an SRT file

Want to keep a copy? Download the movie with its subtitles saved as a standard .srt file that works in any player.

1 00:00:04,200 --> 00:00:06,900 We were never here. 2 00:00:07,400 --> 00:00:10,100 Then why is the door open?

Send it to your Android TV

Pick your TV from the list and the movie opens on the big screen. Your laptop stays free for other things.
